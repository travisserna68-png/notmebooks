Featured Title

Anxiety? Not Me!

Honest. Encouraging. Faith rooted.

In Anxiety? Not Me!, Travis Serna offers a personal and compassionate look at the hidden realities behind anxiety, especially the pressure many people feel to stay silent and appear strong.

Through lived experience, spiritual reflection, and practical encouragement, the book challenges the idea that anxiety is weakness and replaces it with truth, hope, and deeper understanding.

About the Author

Meet Travis Serna

Travis Serna writes with candor, conviction, and compassion. His work speaks to readers who are trying to reconcile strength with vulnerability and faith with emotional honesty.

His writing invites people to step out of performance, reject stigma, and see that their struggle does not define their story.

This website highlights his featured book and the message at the heart of his work: you do not have to fight alone, and anxiety does not get the final word.
